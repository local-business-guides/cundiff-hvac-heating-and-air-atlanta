How a heating repair visit may proceed

The contractor will need to understand the problem and assess the heating equipment. Be ready to explain when the issue began, what you notice, and whether anything has changed since then. After an assessment, ask for a plain explanation of the findings and the work being proposed. You should be able to understand what the recommended next step is before it moves ahead.

Use this checklist before the appointment:

  • Write down the symptoms and when they occur.
  • Mention any changes you’ve noticed in comfort or operation.
  • Clear access to the heating equipment and the area around it.
  • Gather details about past service if you have them.
  • Make a list of questions about the assessment and proposed work.

Avoid opening equipment panels, attempting a repair, or assuming the cause from one symptom. If the system’s behavior changes, update the contractor with that information. If you’re uncertain about what you’re seeing, say so plainly rather than trying to troubleshoot beyond your comfort level.
